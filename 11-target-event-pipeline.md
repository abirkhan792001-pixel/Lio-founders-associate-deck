## The overall brief — why Lio should be in these rooms

Lio sells **labor replacement, not software seats**, so the pipeline concentrates on four room types:

1. **Where DACH industrial CPOs gather in German** (BME/Handelsblatt/Hamburg circuit): trust-based, family-owned buying culture — deals start with conviction in a room, not an RFP.
2. **Where BPO-contract owners gather** (SSON/SIG/Everest/GSA/Deloitte SSC): the 93%-cheaper agentic-BPO wedge needs the people who sign outsourcing renewals — a persona classic procurement events barely reach.
3. **Where US indirect F500 concentrate** (ProcureCon/DPW NY/NAPES/APC): the expansion mandate the Series A was raised for.
4. **Where analysts set buying criteria** (Gartner/Everest/HFS): enterprise buyers de-risk agentic AI through analysts (Gartner itself predicts >40% of agentic projects fail) — analyst-endorsed presence converts skepticism into shortlists.
