## Org, culture, hiring signals

- **Team:** "90+ Lions" (about-us); YC profile says 80, Built In says 92.
- **Values:** Outlier ("S-tier people") · Stay Hungry · Emotional Intelligence · "Get Shit Done" · "Zero Bullshit" (radical transparency). Job-ad pitch: "Silicon Valley ambition and engineering culture in Munich… building one of Europe's leading AI companies."
- **Benefits:** stock options, EGYM Wellpass, meals, Apple equipment, mobility allowance. **All roles Munich on-site** (no remote) except the emerging NYC sales pod.
- **Open roles (Jul 2026):** GTM — Account Executive(s), Sales Engineer, SDR (Munich + New York), Head of US Sales (NYC), Value Strategist/Value Consultant, Product Marketing, Partnership Manager. Engineering — Head of Engineering, Core Engineer, **Forward Deployed Engineer ×2**, Solution Architect, Security/Risk/Compliance Manager, Founders Associates (CEO & CTO office). Implementation — Head of Implementation Engineering, AI Implementation Manager.
- **Read of the hiring pattern (consultant view):** a **Palantir-style forward-deployed model** — FDEs + implementation engineering + value consultants outnumber classic product roles. They even host an "FDE Night 2026." Implication: implementation depth and value-engineering are treated as the product's delivery mechanism and likely moat; also the muscle behind the "<2 weeks to integrate" claim and the "$10M Challenge" value assessments.
- **Security org still maturing:** hiring the first dedicated Security, Risk & Compliance Manager; ISO 27001 certified but **no SOC 2 claim** — relevant for the US enterprise push (see [02](02-product-and-features.md#security--compliance)).
