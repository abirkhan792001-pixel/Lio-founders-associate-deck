← [Index](00-INDEX.md) · Companion: the **[live design canvas](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/f7102cdb-c805-45f6-9676-e241267d395d)** — every slide editable in place, exportable as PNG/PDF. Source artboards: [deck-design/](deck-design/).

**Purpose:** Abir Khan's application deck for the Founders Associate role (CEO & CTO office) at Lio. Theme set by Abir: (1) who I am & why Lio · (2) what I would own from day 0, in one line · (3) why hire me / what Lio gets in return.

**Design source:** Abir's own Zeit AI deck (`Zeit_AI.pdf`, 13 pages, 1920×1080) — the template he supplied. The Lio deck mirrors its system 1:1 so it reads as the same author's body of work.

## Design system (extracted from the template)

| Token | Value | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Bone | `#DCDBCE` | Cover bg, summary boxes, beige chips, dark-slide text |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Content-slide background |
| Ink | `#000000` | All type, heavy rules, black chips, dark slide bg |
| Panel | `#F0F0EE` | Observation/verdict panels, quadrant shading |
| Gray text | `#8A8A82` / `#55554F` | Subtitles / body secondary |
| Hairline | `#E4E4DC` | Row dividers |

- **Type:** single grotesque family (Inter, weights 400–800). Slide titles ~54–58px/-0.02em; cover ~104px; section headers 27px bold over a **3px black rule**; body 16–17px; small-caps letter-spaced labels for eyebrows/footers; author tag letter-spaced 0.38em.
- **Signature elements:** FINDINGS chip (bone bg) vs STRATEGY/LIVE chip (black bg, white text) · verdict block = giant number (88–120px, weight 800) + small-caps label + summary bar with 3px black left border · line-art 8-spoke starburst on cover & thank-you · numbered bold list items (1/2/3) with hairline dividers · monochrome-only data graphics (black + grays, no color) · source footnote in small caps bottom-right.
- **Slide chrome:** cover & thank-you carry the wordmark top-left; content slides top-right. Contact email bottom-right on content slides.
