### The finding: the stated ICP is wider and higher than the evidence

File 10 and the workbook's ICP Analysis tab both state a primary band of **€2–12B revenue, 15,000–50,000 employees**. The eight companies in the modal €1–5B band are not spread across it — they cluster inside a window under two billion euros wide:

**Bullseye: €2.8–4.7B revenue, ~8,000–30,000 employees** (band median 18,050 employees). The €5–20B and €20B+ bands hold a handful of large, hard-won logos rather than a repeatable motion.

**Why it matters for events:** rooms sold on "Global 2000 CPOs" aim a revenue tier above where Lio has actually closed. The German practitioner fairs sit exactly on the proven profile.

### Three segments, unequally proven

1. **DACH industrial core — proven, 14 customers.** Median €3.89B / 19,500 employees. Automotive and discrete manufacturing plus materials/optics/process. SAP estates, 100+ person procurement functions, works councils. Family/foundation ownership means conviction-driven decisions in a room, not an RFP.
2. **Indirect-heavy financial services — proven but thin, 2 customers.** Munich Re and ERGO, and ERGO is Munich Re's subsidiary, so this is arguably one relationship. ~100% indirect spend makes the agentic-BPO arithmetic fastest here. Highest value per logo, least diversified.
3. **US Fortune 500 indirect/GBS — unproven, 0 customers.** The segment the Series A was raised for and the one carrying the heaviest event spend, yet the firmographics table records no US customer. Walmart is logged as an engagement and endorser, explicitly not a customer. Every US room is a bet being placed, not a motion being scaled.

## 2 · Which buyer each room contains

The buying committee has four seats and no single event reaches more than two. **A room is worth what the seat it contains is worth**, and preparation differs per seat.

| Seat | Who | Rooms that contain them |
|---|---|---|
| **Sponsor** | CPO — owns the mandate, wants peer proof, converts in closed rooms | Handelsblatt · WPC · APC · DPW · NAPES · Principals dinners |
| **Economic buyer** | COO/CFO — signs when the BPO or FTE line moves | SSOW ×3 · SIG · SSON San Diego · Deloitte SSC · Maschinenbau-Gipfel |
| **Champion** | Head of Procurement Excellence — runs the evaluation and the pilot | BME eLösungstage · BME Symposium · Procurement Summit · BME Praxisforum |
| **Gatekeeper** | CIO/CISO — cannot say yes, can say no | BME IT-Sourcing · SAP Connect · Gartner symposia |

### The six room types

| Room type | Scale | Seat | Worth |
|---|---|---|---|
| **German practitioner fairs** (BME Symposium, eLösungstage, Procurement Summit, Praxisforum) | 1,000–2,300 | Champion | Exactly the €2.8–4.7B demographic; buyers arrive intending to shop; domestic travel makes it the cheapest fit-per-euro on the calendar |
| **German C-level closed rooms** (Handelsblatt, Principals dinners) | 12–300 | Sponsor | Highest CPO density in Germany; the dinner is Lio's proven signature and the only setting where a family-owned buyer actually decides |
| **GBS / BPO-owner rooms** (SSOW ×3, SSON San Diego, SIG, Deloitte SSC) | 350–1,200 | Economic buyer | The wedge audience — they sign outsourcing renewals, the budget Lio monetises. SIG is the bullseye |
| **US indirect F500** (ProcureCon, DPW NY, NAPES, APC) | 400–1,000 | Sponsor | Director+ rooms one tier above the proven profile; heaviest spend against zero verified customers |
| **Analyst rooms** (Gartner ×3, Everest, HFS) | 100s–3,000 | Gatekeeper | Buyers de-risk agentic AI through analysts; value is the 1:1 briefings, not the floor |
| **Competitor turf** (Zip, Coupa, Ivalua, Jaggaer, Zycus) | n/a | None | Intel only, monitored remotely, never sponsored |

## 3 · Preparation protocol by tier

### P1 — must-own (16 events, ~€736K, 204 staff-days)

| When | Do |
|---|---|
| **T−90** | Book the tier, submit the speaking abstract, enter the award, reserve the dinner venue, settle bundle terms across the organiser's whole portfolio (WBR and SSON each cover 4+ dates) |
| **T−60** | Pull the attendee/exhibitor list and mark every company inside the €2.8–4.7B DACH band plus every GBS/BPO owner. That named list, not the headline attendance figure, is the reason to attend. Send dinner invites against it (8–20 C-level guests) |
| **T−30** | Configure the live agent demo for the industry actually present — an automotive supplier's tail spend is not an insurer's indirect book. Confirm the referenceable customer. Time announcements to the event |
| **T−7** | Brief every person on site on which seat this room contains and which of the two messages applies (agent workforce, or BPO replacement). Lock meetings, dry-run the demo, confirm dinner headcount |
| **On site** | One message, one live-agent demo staffed by an engineer, one dinner. Capture named accounts as they come |
| **T+7** | Fill the pipeline's outcome columns: `Attended`, `Actual cost (€)`, `ICP-fit companies met`, `Qualified meetings`, `Dinner guests`, `Pipeline created (€)`. Log every named company into the firmographics table with revenue band and ownership, so the ICP sharpens per event. Run follow-ups within the week |
| **T+30** | Set the pipeline's `Verdict` — Renew / Renew at lower tier / Attend-only next time / Drop / Too early — and stamp `Outcome logged`. That verdict, read against `Actual cost`, decides next year's tier |

### P2 — strong fit (31 events, selective)

- **T−60:** answer one question — does this room contain a seat no P1 event already reaches this quarter? If it duplicates covered ground, skip and put the money into the P1 dinner. Most P2 events fail this test; that is the point of the tier.
- **T−30:** attend, don't exhibit. One or two people with a meeting schedule, no booth. A booth is earned only after an attend-only year produced named pipeline.
- **Cluster rule:** never travel for a single P2. London September week reaches three in one trip; the Berlin November weeks two apiece. A P2 that can't attach to a cluster or a P1 is a skip.
- **T+14:** set `Verdict` and stamp `Outcome logged` in the pipeline — whether the room held what it promised is what promotes an event to P1 or drops it to P3.

### P3 — opportunistic (13 events, no spend)

- Monitor competitor conferences remotely via one named person; no booth, no ticket, no exception.
- Walk the floor only at near-zero marginal cost (booth already next door, team already in the city).
- Re-verify the dormant list quarterly so freed budget stays deliberate rather than forgotten.

## 4 · What the plan implies

- **Weight the German practitioner fairs at least as heavily as the US circuit.** They sit on the proven profile, reach the champion who runs the evaluation, and are the cheapest fit per euro. The US rooms aim a tier higher and cost more — a defensible bet for the Series A mandate, but it should be recognised and measured as a bet.
- **Attach a conversion measure to every US event before the next renewal cycle.** 9+ US events since Nov 2025, zero verified US customers. Not yet a failure — the beachhead is young — but without recorded outcomes there's no evidence base for the 2027 renewals already being decided.
- **Protect the dinners before anything else.** ~€120K of the programme is ten sidecar dinners, the only format that reliably reaches the sponsor seat in the setting a family-owned buyer decides in. Cut a booth before a dinner.
- **Feed events back into the ICP.** The firmographics table's second block — 13 companies seen at the Clubhouse edition (SWM, NEW AG, Pfalzwerke, Deutsche Bahn, Lufthansa, FlixBus, Webasto, Benteler…) — has never had revenue or headcount verified. A few hours of work would either confirm a fourth ICP cluster or close the question.

## Known limits

Insurance revenue is IFRS 17 and not directly comparable to industrial revenue. The "Förster" logo is recorded with its identity unverified and an estimated revenue dating from 2017. The 13-company Clubhouse block is industry classification only and is excluded from every figure above. Post-event outcome tracking — the gap that made every renewal an argument from memory — now exists as eight columns on the pipeline (see [file 11](11-target-event-pipeline.md)), but they are empty by design: the evidence base starts accumulating with the September events, so the first renewals it can actually inform are the 2027 ones.
