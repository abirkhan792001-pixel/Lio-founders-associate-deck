- **US pod:** Petras ~8 (owns SIG, NAPES, PC West, San Diego), Schmid 4–5; every US booth staffed with 1 SE/FDE running live agent demos.
- **DACH squad:** 2–3 AEs per German fair; FDE demo stations at eLösungstage/Procurement Summit/DPW; Berlin November = one 10-day deployment.
- **Marketing:** Events lead runs all 10 dinners + booth ops; PMM covers analyst events (AR meetings, content capture).
- **Load:** 204 on-site staff-days + ~30% prep ≈ 265 days ≈ 1.2 FTE-years across the GTM team.
- **Hiring triggers:** (1) US field-marketing/events contractor from Jan 2027 — Mar–Jun 2027 runs 7 US/UK/EU events parallel to DACH spring; (2) second Sales Engineer by Q2-2027. Without both, Q2-2027 breaks the current team.
